Decision

Use the 026 family. Run one small test first.

026 is the best offline policy in the current evidence set. Use its causal trunk, semantic action embeddings, temporal decoder, and group decoder.

Do not treat 026 as a clean ablation. The change from 024 to 026 changed many variables. Experiment A must separate trunk width from decoder structure.

Strong evidence

026 is the best scale candidate. It has the best stock result under the 026 evaluation protocol. It also has practical four-frame execution.

Moderate evidence

Structured action decoding helps. Experiments 013, 019, 024, and 026 support this result. The comparisons are not fully controlled.

Strong evidence

Do not scale the tested Q methods. Experiments 027 and 030 fail in closed-loop control.

Compute note. A 10× larger model with 10× more tokens needs about 100× more dense compute. The estimate is 9.3 EFLOP. A strict 10×-compute option appears in section 8.

Main protocol

Use 96 character boots against a level-9 CPU. Each match has 7,200 frames. Each restart uses a random legal stage.

Use net stocks per minute and its boot-clustered confidence interval. Use net damage as the second metric.

Model 026

Training uses true actions. Inference uses sampled actions.

This difference creates exposure bias. The decoder sees correct prefixes during training. It sees its own sampled prefixes during play.

AWR and Q losses

Important confounds

  • 024 and 026 change width, context, codec, offsets, decoder, and training exposure.
  • 013 changes token count and needs four full trunk calls per action.
  • 028 trains on about twice the examples of 026.
  • 029 changes loss, model size, throughput, and wall time.
  • Old evaluations use different match rules. Do not mix them with the 026 protocol.

Most likely limits

  1. Rare action transitions cause exposure errors.
  2. The policy needs structured capacity for joint action trajectories.
  3. The data needs more diversity and better rare-event coverage.
  4. The larger model can exceed the reaction-time limit.

Longer context, more hand-built features, and a larger generic output head have negative evidence.
